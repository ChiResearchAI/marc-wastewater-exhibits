Finding. The two bases differ by roughly twelve orders of magnitude, and in overlapping months there is not one shared sample date between them. Nothing can be averaged, trended or thresholded across the pair without an explicit, recorded conversion — and the basis changes partway through the site's own record. A common data model that does not carry the basis as a typed field loses the series silently.

What that implies for the data model

RESPONSE

The normalization science is unsettled and the region is already split. So the platform's job is not to choose a formula — it is to make the choice explicit, reversible and auditable.

01

Immutable raw capture

The un-normalized measurement is the system of record, with write-once storage. Nothing downstream can overwrite what the laboratory reported.

02

Typed normalization inputs

Flow, served population with its basis and vintage, every indicator measured, assay platform, extraction method, recovery control, detection and quantification limits, sample matrix, grab or composite — each a queryable field, none privileged.

03

Versioned semantic layer

Normalization and baseline windows are named, versioned transformations computed at read time. Changing the method publishes a new version; it does not rewrite history.

04

Published metric with lineage

Every figure on the dashboard expands to the records behind it, the transformation applied, and any exclusions with their reason codes.

Why this matters here. MARC has asked to host and maintain the system independently after the project period. An architecture that stores conclusions needs a consultant every time the method moves. One that stores measurements and versions the method does not.

Limits of this exhibit

CANDOUR

What it does not show

Nothing here measures disease prevalence, and the level rule applied is Kansas's published convention used as a worked example — not a MARC decision. Opioid targets, which the RFP includes, are absent from this dataset.

What we could not determine

Which assay platforms the Kansas and Missouri laboratories use, which materially affects comparability. Missouri's current dashboard methodology at the detail Kansas publishes. The NWSS data dictionary's own field definitions, which we did not open and therefore do not cite.

What we would ask for first

MARC's own sampling and laboratory methodology documentation from member utilities and state laboratory partners, and sewershed boundary and served-population data with its basis and vintage.
